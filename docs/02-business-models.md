# Business Models

## Revenue paths considered

### 1. Direct B2B production service — current favorite

Sell finished miniature/AI-native short-form videos to realtors, teams, builders, developers, brokers, resorts, dealerships, or other high-ticket sellers.

Why it is attractive:

- revenue does not depend on huge audience scale
- the buyer already understands the value of marketing media
- a handful of clients can reach $1,000/month
- each client project can also become portfolio/social content with permission

Illustrative pricing to test, not validated pricing:

| Offer | Test price |
|---|---:|
| 15-second miniature listing | $79 |
| 30-second miniature listing | $149 |
| 45-60 second premium piece | $249 |
| 4 listings/month | $399-$599 |
| Team/brokerage package | $1,000+/month |

Pricing should be validated with real buyers before being treated as fact.

## 2. Affiliate channel

Produce miniature unboxing/demonstration videos of real products and link to the actual products.

Better economics come from:

- high-value products
- manufacturer/direct affiliate programs
- software or services connected to the product ecosystem
- recurring commissions where possible

Do not assume Amazon alone makes the model attractive, especially for electronics with modest commission rates.

### Example math

If average commission is $50, $1,000/month requires 20 sales.

Illustrative funnel only:

- 2% of viewers click
- 5% of clickers buy
- viewer-to-sale rate = 0.1%
- ~20,000 commercially relevant views could generate ~20 sales

These are hypothetical conversion assumptions, not benchmarks.

## 3. YouTube/platform ad revenue

Possible but less attractive as the primary thesis.

At an illustrative effective revenue of $5 per 1,000 views, $1,000/month would require roughly 200,000 monetized views/month.

Actual RPM varies widely by geography, niche, format, season, and advertiser demand.

## 4. Influence -> own offer/leads

Potentially the best long-term economics.

The miniature content can attract attention while the real monetization comes from:

- lead generation
- service sales
- consulting
- list building
- referral partnerships
- sponsorships
- owned products

For real estate, public content can become customer acquisition for the production service itself.

## 5. Sponsorships

Possible sponsors around real-estate media include:

- mortgage companies
- title companies
- home inspectors
- moving companies
- home-security providers
- builders/remodelers
- home-warranty providers
- furniture/smart-home brands

## 6. Brokerage/developer subscription

Potentially higher-value than selling one video at a time.

Example value proposition:

> Every new listing receives a branded social-content pack, with one signature miniature format plus multiple derivative assets.

A developer with dozens of units or a brokerage with consistent listing volume could justify much higher monthly spend than an individual agent.

## Current prioritization

1. **Real-estate/high-ticket B2B service**
2. **Recurring team/developer packages**
3. **Audience + lead-generation flywheel**
4. **Affiliate revenue**
5. **Sponsorships**
6. **Platform ad revenue**

## Core economic principle

Optimize for **revenue per useful viewer/client interaction**, not raw views.

A 20,000-view video that reaches buyers or agents can be more valuable than a 500,000-view novelty clip with no monetization path.
