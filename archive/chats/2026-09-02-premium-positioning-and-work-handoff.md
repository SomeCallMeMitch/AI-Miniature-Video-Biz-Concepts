# Session Summary — Premium Positioning and Work Handoff

**Date:** 2026-09-02

## What changed

The project remains focused on validating miniature/AI-native real-estate listing media, but a new go-to-market hypothesis was introduced.

### Premium/exclusivity hypothesis

Instead of broadly marketing a low-priced service with a public website, the business may eventually be stronger as a scarce, high-end creative service for agents handling multi-million-dollar listings.

Possible reasons:

- higher fees can support expensive, high-touch production
- exclusivity may itself have value to luxury agents
- fewer clients are needed to reach meaningful revenue
- less public marketing may reduce how quickly competitors copy the positioning/workflow

This is a hypothesis, not a final decision.

### Warm San Diego test path

The user has a niece who is a realtor in San Diego and connected with an office that markets properties in the low-million-dollar range.

If technical production quality passes, this relationship may be useful for:

- permissioned access to listing assets
- private prototype testing
- candid realtor feedback
- early willingness-to-pay/value conversations
- testing whether exclusivity matters

Do not treat this relationship as proof of demand.

## Mode / workflow decision

For the immediate next phase, use a substantial Work/Deep Research session to conduct a September 2026 production-feasibility study.

Primary research objective:

> Identify the strongest current workflow for producing premium miniature real-estate videos from real listing assets while preserving property identity.

Compare:

1. best-quality premium stack
2. lowest-cost commercially viable stack

Research should cover fidelity, reference consistency, hand interaction, image-to-video, multi-shot consistency, cost, API access, and commercial-use terms.

## Durable-memory decision

GitHub remains the project system of record.

The chat-handoff protocol was strengthened so substantial Chat, Work, Deep Research, and Codex sessions must write useful findings back to the repository when possible rather than leaving important knowledge only in chat history.
