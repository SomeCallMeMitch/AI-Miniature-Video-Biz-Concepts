# Animation contact and review lessons

Added reusable production guidance from a separate deterministic 3D animation workstream: action planning, local grip anchors, swept arm/head/product clearance, hinge mechanics, visible entrances/exits and final-encode review.

The ten reviewed design skills are mapped to production uses in [the guide](../../docs/10-animation-contact-and-review.md). UX Animation, Polish and Explainers Assets contribute useful methods. Several other skills target UI or unrelated visual formats and offer little direct benefit to a rounded 3D product film. None supplies a collision-safe character rig by itself.

This public record intentionally contains general guidance only. Private branded films, image collections and source archives are not copied here pending the owner’s decision about public visibility. The separate Blender V07 product pilot and September real-estate decisions remain intact.

No new render, visual certification, model benchmark or market validation occurred in this archival task. Next: apply the contact-review skill to one staged interaction and retain the actual preview evidence before committing to a full movie.
